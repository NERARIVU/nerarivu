About Us

Nerarivu Samoohika Samskarika Sangadan is a charitable society registered with the District Registrar, Alappuzha. Established on 15 February 2023, the organisation works for the welfare and development of the community through educational, social, cultural, health, environmental and awareness programmes.

Vision

To build a healthy, educated, compassionate and culturally enriched society where every individual can live with dignity, participate in community life and contribute positively to society.

Mission

  • Anti-drug awareness and prevention programmes.
  • Education, scientific awareness and learning opportunities.
  • Welfare and social inclusion of senior citizens.
  • Arts, culture, literature and creative activities.
  • Social welfare and community development.
  • Youth participation, volunteerism and responsible citizenship.
